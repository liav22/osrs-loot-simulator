# Brimstone chest

Source: [Brimstone chest — Possible loot](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Brimstone_chest?oldid=15338725#Possible_loot),
revision **15338725** (20 September 2026), page ID 210246. The shared fish
calculation comes from `Module:Slayer chest fish chart`, revision **15317734**.

One attempt is **one opening**. A unique pre-roll awards Broken dragon hasta at
1/200 or one of five dusk mystic pieces at 1/1000 each. Those outcomes are one
mutually-exclusive selection behind a 1/100 pre-roll, preserving every listed
marginal rate. A unique replaces the main reward.

The ordinary main rows occupy 57 of 60 slots. The remaining 3/60 are the
published 1/20 fish branch, folded into the same weighted table so every
non-unique opening yields exactly one main or fish reward.

Fish probabilities reproduce the wiki module's ordered Fishing-level rolls.
Raw shark, shark lure and sea turtle unlock at level 17; raw manta ray unlocks
at 33. High-tier rolls use the module's 2/8 fish, 3/8 raw-shark and 3/8
shark-lure split. Shark-lure quantities roll the same 80–250 base range as raw
sharks and double it, so only even quantities from 160 through 500 can occur.

The document is generated and `verified`. Regression coverage checks the
unique and main denominators, the fish branch's 1/20 total at representative
Fishing levels, exact quantity support, one-reward-per-opening behavior and
seeded simulation.
